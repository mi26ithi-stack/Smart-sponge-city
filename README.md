# 🌊 Smart Sponge City
> **An AI-IoT Based Intelligent System for Stormwater Management, Groundwater Recharge, Flood Prediction, and Water-Body Safety**

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Rapid urbanisation has replaced natural permeable surfaces with concrete, leading to urban flooding, waterlogging, and reduced groundwater recharge. 

The **Smart Sponge City** project combines conventional sponge-city infrastructure (permeable surfaces, rain gardens, recharge pits) with real-time sensing, **Internet of Things (IoT)**, automated water routing, and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** for proactive flood prediction and efficient water management.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Architecture

The system operates across **5 Interconnected Levels**:
1. **Absorb**: Permeable pavements, rain gardens, and vegetated infiltration beds.
2. **Store**: Rooftop rainwater harvesting and underground/detention storage tanks.
3. **Recharge**: Recharge pits and trenches to replenish groundwater aquifers.
4. **Monitor**: Continuous IoT sensing of rainfall intensity, multi-depth soil moisture, storage levels, and pond rate-of-rise.
5. **Predict & Respond**: AI-driven risk assessment (Normal, Caution, Warning, Critical) to automatically control water routing valves and trigger early flood warnings.

---

## 🔬 Experimental Methodology & Parameters
The prototype compares a **Conventional Urban System** vs. a **Sponge Urban System** across various parameters:
- **Runoff vs. Infiltration Rates** under varied surface permeability.
- **Soil Saturation Dynamics** monitored at multiple depths.
- **Pond Water-Level Dynamics** analyzing both absolute depth and rate-of-rise to detect hazardous conditions early.
- **Automated Water Routing** based on soil saturation and available storage capacity.
